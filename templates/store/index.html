Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our jars, storage & delivery.

How long do Pickpickles jars last & how should I store them?

Our natural vinegar & salt brine keeps the jar completely safe during delivery transit. Upon delivery, always keep the jar in the refrigerator. When refrigerated, our pickles stay ultra-crunchy, tangy, and fresh for up to 2 months! Always use a clean, dry utensil when serving.

What ingredients are used in Pickpickles?

We hand-select crisp local cucumbers, sweet carrots, crunchy bell peppers, ruby beetroots, deshi onions, fresh green chillies, and juicy fruits like golden pineapples, ripe mangoes, seedless green grapes, and fresh quail eggs. Brined in filtered water, pure cane vinegar, Himalayan pink salt, brown cane sugar, fresh garlic cloves, dill herbs, bay leaves, yellow mustard seeds, black peppercorn, and crushed red chili flakes. 100% oil-free and handcrafted with love!

What is the delivery timeline and shipping charge?

🥒 Fresh Batch Preparation (3 Days): We craft our pickles fresh upon order. Every jar requires at least 3 days of natural brining & infusion to develop that signature crunchy snap and balanced tangy-sweet flavor.

🚚 Delivery Transit (2–3 Days): Once properly brined, your jar is bubble-wrapped and dispatched via courier (takes 2–3 days).

💵 Delivery Charges: FREE Delivery when you order 6 or more jars! For orders of 1–5 jars, delivery is a flat ৳150 nationwide across Bangladesh (Inside & Outside Dhaka). Total estimated time from order confirmation to your doorstep is 5 to 6 days.

Can I pay via bKash or Cash on Delivery?

Yes! We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) as well as direct bKash payments. You can select your preferred method during checkout.
