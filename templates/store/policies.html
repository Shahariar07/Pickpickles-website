Clause 2.0

Official Payment Policy & Accepted Payment Methods

COD & bKash Only

2.1 Accepted Payment Options: Pickpickles exclusively operates with two verified payment options: Cash on Delivery (COD) and bKash (Send Money). We do not accept or offer any other payment channels (such as credit/debit cards, bank wire transfers, or third-party gateways).

💵 Cash on Delivery (COD) Pay at Delivery
  • Zero Advance Payment: No prepayment, advance booking deposit, or verification fee is required for Cash on Delivery.
  • Payment Upon Delivery: Pay the full invoice total in cash directly to the courier delivery rider upon receiving and inspecting your parcel at your doorstep.
  • Courier Coverage Availability: Cash on Delivery is supported in all locations where our courier service partners maintain active delivery coverage.
bKash bKash Payment Advance Payment Required
  • Mandatory Advance Payment: When choosing bKash, 100% of the invoice amount must be paid in advance via "Send Money" before your order is processed for packing.
  • Official bKash Account: Send Money strictly to our official bKash personal number as displayed securely on the checkout page during order placement.
  • TrxID Verification: Provide your sending bKash mobile number and the Transaction ID (TrxID) in the checkout form for instant order verification and dispatch clearance.
Payment Security & Fraud Notice:

Always verify the official bKash number directly on our secure checkout page. Never send funds to any external agent or unverified personal phone numbers.

2.1 Inspection Right: Customers are strongly advised to inspect the exterior condition of the parcel in the presence of the delivery rider before taking full possession.

2.2 Visible Transit Damage: If the outer parcel is crushed, punctured, or leaking liquid, you have the full right to reject the delivery on the spot without paying any money (for Cash on Delivery orders).

Clause 4.0

Transit Damage & 100% Free Replacement Guarantee

Zero Customer Liability

Pickpickles packages all products in premium food-grade airtight sealed jars engineered for maximum freshness, hygiene, and durability. In the rare event of transit damage, jar cracking, or seal failure caused by courier mishandling:

Official Resolution Protocol for Damaged Jars:

  1. Capture Evidence: Take 1–2 clear photographs or a short video showing the damaged jar or compromised seal along with the courier shipping label.
  2. Notify Us: Send the photos along with your Order Number (e.g. PKP-00001) or Phone Number directly to our WhatsApp (01794271523) or Facebook inbox within 24 hours of parcel receipt.
  3. Zero-Cost Resolution: Pickpickles will immediately dispatch a brand new, fresh airtight jar at 100% zero extra cost (including free re-delivery), or process a full refund to your bKash account if preferred. No disputes, no return fee.
Clause 5.0

Airtight Cap Seal & Minor Brine Seepage Protocol

4.1 Understanding Natural Brine Pressure: Our pickles are packed in airtight sealed jars with artisanal vinegar brines and aromatic mustard oils. Long-distance road vibration may occasionally release a few drops of liquid around the safety seal.

Recommended Action:

  • If Jar Body is Intact: Wipe the exterior jar with a damp cloth, rinse under clean water, dry with a paper towel, and place directly into the refrigerator. The airtight hermetic environment keeps the product 100% sterile, crisp, and fresh.
  • If Cap is Dislodged or Seal Broken: If the airtight seal has completely popped open causing significant loss of contents, photograph the jar and contact support for an immediate free replacement.
Clause 6.0

Incorrect Product or Missing Item Protocol

5.1 Quality Control Assurance: Every order is cross-checked 3 times before sealing. If an error occurs:

Free Doorstep Correction:

  • Incorrect Flavor: Notify us with a photo of the received jar. We will dispatch the correct flavor immediately and arrange a courier exchange at our expense.
  • Missing Jar: The omitted jar will be dispatched immediately via expedited courier at zero shipping charge.
Clause 7.0

Food Quality, Shelf-Life & Refrigeration Guidelines

6.1 Clean Formulation: Pickpickles products contain zero synthetic preservatives, zero commercial food chemicals, and zero artificial coloring. Preservation is naturally achieved via pure cane vinegar and Himalayan pink salt.

6.2 Mandatory Refrigeration: All jars MUST BE REFRIGERATED immediately upon delivery. Chilling preserves the audible crunch and prevents over-fermentation.

6.3 Hygiene Practices: Always use a clean, dry stainless steel spoon when serving. Never introduce moisture, wet utensils, or fingers into the jar.

6.4 Quality Feedback Window: If you notice any unusual quality defect within 48 hours of delivery, please contact our quality desk with batch details for immediate assistance.

Clause 8.0

Refund Policy & bKash Settlement

7.1 Eligibility for Monetary Refund: Refunds are granted when an order is cancelled prior to courier dispatch, or in verified cases of transit damage where the customer explicitly prefers a refund over a free replacement jar.

7.2 Processing Timeline: Approved refunds are sent directly to the customer's personal bKash account within 24 to 48 business hours.

7.3 Zero Fee Deduction: 100% of the eligible payment is refunded without any processing or transactional deductions.

Clause 9.0

Order Cancellation & Address Amendment Policy

8.1 Pre-Dispatch Changes: Customers may modify delivery addresses, update contact phone numbers, or cancel orders free of charge within 12 hours of placing the order, before courier manifest generation.

8.2 Post-Dispatch Clause: Once an order has been packaged and handed over to Pathao or Steadfast courier with an active tracking code, in-transit cancellation is strictly prohibited.
