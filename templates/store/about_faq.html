Our Pickling History & Craft

Our Pickling History & Craft in Bangladesh

Inspired by classic vinegar-brined deli pickling traditions, we craft pure, crunchy, and oil-free pickles across Bangladesh!

🥒

1. Fresh Local Market Veggies

We hand-select firm, crisp cucumbers, sweet carrots, crisp bell peppers, deshi onions, green chillies, and fresh aromatics directly from local markets for maximum crunch.

🌿

2. Artisanal Vinegar Brine

Zero heavy mustard oil. We use pure cane vinegar, whole mustard seeds, fragrant bay leaves, Himalayan pink salt, and fresh local garlic.

⚡

3. Cold-Snap Ferment

Our controlled brining process locks in cellular crispness, so every spear gives that iconic, audible "crunch" when you bite.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our jars, storage & delivery.

How long do Pickpickles jars last & how should I store them?

Our natural vinegar & salt brine keeps the jar completely safe during delivery transit. Upon delivery, always keep the jar in the refrigerator. When refrigerated, our pickles stay ultra-crunchy, tangy, and fresh for up to 1 month! Always use a clean, dry utensil when serving.

What is the delivery timeline and shipping charge?

🥒 Fresh Batch Preparation (3 Days): We craft our pickles fresh upon order. Every jar requires at least 3 days of natural brining & infusion to develop that signature crunchy snap and balanced tangy-sweet flavor.

🚚 Delivery Transit (2–3 Days): Once properly brined, your jar is bubble-wrapped and dispatched via courier (takes 2–3 days).

💵 Delivery Charges: Flat ৳150 across all of Bangladesh. Total estimated time from order confirmation to your doorstep is 5 to 6 days.

What ingredients are used in Pickpickles?
We hand-select crisp local cucumbers, sweet carrots, crisp bell peppers, deshi onions, fresh green chillies, filtered water, pure cane vinegar, Himalayan pink salt, fresh garlic cloves, bay leaves, and whole yellow mustard seeds. 100% oil-free and handcrafted with love!
Can I pay via bKash / Nagad or Cash on Delivery?

Yes! We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) as well as direct bKash and Nagad payments. You can select your preferred method during checkout.
