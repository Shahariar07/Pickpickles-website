Our Pickling History & Craft

Our Pickling History & Craft in Bangladesh

Inspired by classic vinegar-brined deli traditions & authentic local orchard flavors, we handcraft pure, crunchy, and oil-free vegetable & seasonal fruit pickles across Bangladesh!

🥒

1. Farm-Fresh Veggies & Fruits

We hand-select crisp local cucumbers, sweet carrots, colorful bell peppers, beetroots, deshi onions, spicy chillies, and luscious fruits like golden pineapples, ripe mangoes & juicy green grapes.

🌿

2. Artisanal Vinegar & Herb Brine

Zero oil. We use pure cane vinegar, whole yellow mustard seeds, fragrant bay leaves, dill herbs, Himalayan pink salt, garlic cloves, black peppercorn & crushed chili flakes & others.

⚡

3. Cold-Snap & Natural Brining

Our controlled brining process locks in cellular crispness, so every veggie spear and juicy fruit bite delivers that signature audible snap and sweet-tangy zest.

🍔

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our jars, storage & delivery.

What is the shelf life of the pickles and how to store them?

Our natural vinegar & salt brine keeps the jar completely safe during delivery transit. Upon delivery, please kepp the pickle refrigerated at all times for lasting freshness, quality and flavour. As our pickles are 100% homemade and free from preservatives, we recommend consuming them within 1 month for the best taste and crunch. Always use a clean, dry spoon and keep the lid thightly closed after each use.

What is the delivery timeline and shipping charge?

🥒 Fresh Batch Preparation (3 Days): We craft our pickles fresh upon order. Every jar requires at least 3 days of natural brining & infusion to develop that signature crunchy snap and balanced tangy-sweet flavor.

🚚 Delivery Transit (2–3 Days): Once properly brined, your jar is bubble-wrapped and dispatched via courier (takes 2–3 days).

💵 Delivery Charges: Flat ৳150 nationwide across Bangladesh (Inside & Outside Dhaka). Total estimated time from order confirmation to your doorstep is 5 to 6 days.
