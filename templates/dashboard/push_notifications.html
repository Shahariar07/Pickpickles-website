How to Install the PWA on Android Phone

1

Open in Chrome

Open your Android phone's Google Chrome browser and navigate to: /dashboard/

2

Install to Home Screen

Tap the Chrome menu (the 3 vertical dots ⋮ in the top right) and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

3

Tap Subscribe

Open the installed app icon from your phone home screen, come to this page, and tap "Subscribe to Push Messaging".

Pro-Tip for Android: Make sure Chrome notifications are enabled under your phone's Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Chrome ➔ Notifications, and Battery Optimization is set to Unrestricted so alerts wake your screen instantly.
